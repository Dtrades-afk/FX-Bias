    Checking saved readings…

    Prices are ECB daily reference rates from Frankfurter, not your broker’s prices. Gold and silver are logged but not checked, because no price source is connected for them. The log lives only in this browser, so it grows only on days you open the site on this device. Small samples can mislead.

    Methodology

    Each currency gets a score from −100 to +100, the average of the inputs that loaded. Every input counts as +1, 0 or −1 (equal weights, untested):

    • Fundamentals: policy-rate trend, inflation trend and 2-year government yield trend (yields currently cover USD, EUR and CAD only).
    • Sentiment: speculative positioning from the weekly CFTC COT report (halved when positioning is crowded), market risk tone from the VIX and S&P 500, and AI-scored news headlines when the news keys are set.

    A pair’s bias is the base score minus the quote score, halved. Gaps of +25 and +50 or more read Bullish and Very bullish, −25 and −50 or less read Bearish and Very bearish, and anything between is Neutral. Gold and silver use positioning only, damped, against the dollar’s score.

    Data comes from FRED, the CFTC, the ECB, the Bank of Canada, Finnhub and Myfxbook (retail positioning, shown for context and not scored), and refreshes when you open the site. Macro data lags by weeks and COT by days. The 7D trend lines and the scorecard come from readings saved in your browser. This is a decision aid and not financial advice.
